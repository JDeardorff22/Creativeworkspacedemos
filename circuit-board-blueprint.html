Called by: Stage 1 (user/OUs), Stage 2 (creations, inspirations),

Stage 5 (append messages), Stage 6 (query library)
🤖 PraaS API (Single Instance — Azure APIM)
Endpoint: praas.azure-api.net/prompt-service
Auth: Azure AD (ClientSecretCredential) + Ocp-Apim-Subscription-Key
Body: Content-Type text/plain (base64-encoded JSON)

Prompts used:
• ics-image-generation-v2 (Stage 4)
• ics-image-optimization-v2 (Stage 5)
• ics-video-generation (Stage 5 video)
• ics-concept-generation, ics-brief, ics-script (other modules)

Called by: Stage 4 (generate) + Stage 5 (refine/video)
☁️ Google Cloud Storage (Single Bucket)
Bucket: ics-create-{env}
Stores: generated images, generated videos
Access: Signed URLs (⚠️ expire in 30 minutes)

Called by: Stage 4 (upload new images),
Stage 5 (upload refined images/videos),
Stage 6 (serve thumbnails in library)
