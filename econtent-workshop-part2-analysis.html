Immediate Pain Point Solves

1

Content Discrepancy Between PIM and Live Retailer Site

3–4 voices

Users have no efficient way to compare content that's been submitted/approved in PIM against what's actually live on retailer PDPs. When leadership asks "why is this wrong?" the brand teams respond "it's right in PIM" — but the content on the live retailer site is outdated or incorrect. There is no clear ownership of who closes the gap (brand side vs. customer team side), and no system to surface the root cause of discrepancies.

"I get the same pings... we're like, it's right in PIM, like why is it wrong?"
Raised by: Brand team member (Matt / Old Spice) Epic: Content Integrity
2

Artwork & CIC Process Visibility and Ownership Clarity

4–5 voices

The artwork creation and CIC (Creative in Context) process is excessively long, frequently late, and it's unclear who owns each step across RBU, BFO, and brand teams. Users expressed fear about the timeline and said they don't know where SKUs are in the pipeline. Last year, a tracking list showed where each SKU was in the artwork process — users want this again.

"I'm already scared of looking at that path"
Raised by: Old Spice, Olay teams + Riley Ellingson Epic: Pipeline Tracking
3

Content Variation Turnaround Time with CMG

2 voices

After creating a hero SKU's content, getting CMG to produce the 12+ variations takes over a month of back-and-forth. This blocks the entire collection from being content-complete. Users need a faster, more predictable variation workflow.

Raised by: Old Spice / Secret representative Epic: Workflow Automation
4

MOI Rejection Inconsistency Across Retailers

3 voices

Main Operating Images (MOIs) with visual cues/banners are accepted or rejected inconsistently across Amazon, Walmart, and Target — and even inconsistently at the SKU level within a single retailer. Users are forced to create two versions of every MOI (one with visual cues, one without) as a fallback. There's an active issue sheet with Amazon on this topic.

Raised by: Ben (presenter) + team members Epic: Asset Management
5

GEO/LLM Playbook Timing for JFM 27 Pipeline

2 voices

Hundreds of SKUs are entering the pipeline for August/September e-content readiness. Brand teams need the GEO optimization playbook rolled out NOW so they can incorporate it into copy being written today — not after it's already been submitted. Marketing copy takes significant time due to many codes and considerations.

Raised by: Riley Ellingson Epic: Content Strategy
🟡 Important

Important Improvements

6

Retailer Content Dashboard Access & Metrics Customization

3–4 voices

Corporate data analytics has built prototype dashboards for Amazon, Walmart, and Target that scrape live PDP content, score it against retailer-specific criteria, and show performance metrics (click share, conversion share, relevancy). Users want access to test these, and want to influence which metrics are displayed (e.g., glance views, conversion rate). Currently refreshes are ad hoc — users want regular refresh cycles.

"a game changer" — "our band 5's asked for that a lot"
Raised by: Ben (presenter) + multiple attendees Epic: Analytics & Reporting
7

Cross-Retailer Content Consistency View

2 voices

Users want a single unified view showing how one SKU's content appears across Amazon, Walmart, and Target simultaneously — to quickly identify where content is inconsistent across retailers without checking 3 separate dashboards.

Raised by: Ben (presenter) Epic: Analytics & Reporting
8

Content Score Change Tracking with Causal Attribution

2 voices

When content scores change over time on the retailer dashboards, users want to see what specific content change caused the score change — and whether the change was positive or negative. The prototype has this partially built with "major/minor change" tracking.

"pressure test different MOI formats based on how well it performs"
Raised by: Ben + Old Spice team Epic: Analytics & Reporting
9

Backend Search Term Audit for LLM Hallucination Prevention

1–2 voices

Old claims in backend search terms (not visible to consumers) are causing AI assistants like Alexa to hallucinate incorrect product information. Users need a standardized checklist/audit process to ensure backend search terms are current and aligned with on-site content.

Raised by: Sean (GEO presenter) Epic: Content Strategy
10

Education for Leadership on Retailer Realities

2–3 voices

Leadership makes asks (e.g., "update all MOIs to Wild" or "why doesn't top 100 look perfect?") without understanding retailer-specific limitations, rejection rates, and inconsistencies. Users want structured education pushed UP to leadership so expectations are realistic.

Raised by: Old Spice representative Epic: Enablement
🟢 Nice-to-Have

Future Enhancements

11

AI-Powered Content Validation (ChatGPT Assistant with Playbook)

1–2 voices

A user suggested building a ChatGPT-based assistant that has the GEO playbook loaded, where brand teams can plug in their copy and get instant validation against best practices — reducing dependency on Sean for manual reviews.

Raised by: Unidentified team member Epic: AI/Automation
12

Text-Based A+ Content Templates for LLM Readability

2 voices

Historically, A+ (enhanced) content has been image-heavy and therefore invisible to LLMs that scrape PDPs. Users need text-based A+ content templates with comparison tables, FAQ sections, and claims that are readable by Alexa/ChatGPT.

Raised by: Sean (GEO presenter) Epic: Content Strategy
13

Brand Story Module as Quick Win for LLM Visibility

1 voice

The brand story module at the bottom of PDPs can be applied brand-wide (one-time setup) with text-based content that LLMs can scrape. This is an easy win that doesn't require per-SKU customization.

Raised by: Sean (GEO presenter) Epic: Content Strategy
14

Epoch/A-B Testing Built into Content Workflow

1 voice

The timeline references "executing epoch testing" on initial content before variating across SKUs — but there's no formal process or tool integration for this. Users would benefit from structured A/B testing between base content creation and full variation.

Raised by: Ben (presenter) Epic: Workflow Automation

Key Observations for Create Studio Relevance
